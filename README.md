# Offline-Internet
Me(and Chatgpt) have built this personal internet where you and only you can access and it's all yours. It runs on HTML so it's easily editable (if you know coding). It has 4 main pages: Music Story Finder, Human Algorithm, Personal Wikipedia and Internet Museum as well as the home page.


How Each One works:
### 1. Music Story Finder

This is pretty much your personal music database.

Instead of relying on Spotify or Apple Music to organise your music , you can build your own library. Every song can have information attached to it, including:

- Song
- Artist
- Curator
- Album
- URL
- Playlist
- Decade
- File

You can search through your entire music library, add new songs and export the database as a JSON file.

The idea is that this becomes a permanent record of your music taste that isn't tied to one music service.

### 2. Human Algorithm

The Human Algorithm is for discovering music through people instead of algorithms.

Rather than Spotify deciding what you should listen to, you can keep track of recommendations that come from actual humans — friends, family, creators, DJs, musicians, or anyone else.

It's basically your own music discovery network.

### 3. Personal Wikipedia

Your Personal Wikipedia is your own personal knowledge base.

You can use it to store information about anything you want to remember — people, places, ideas, projects, interests, facts, stories, or basically anything else.

It's your own version of Wikipedia, except you're the one creating and controlling it.

### 4. Internet Museum

The Internet Museum is a place to preserve things from the internet that you don't want to disappear.

Websites, videos, images, memes, articles, creators, old internet culture, or anything else you think is worth remembering can be added here.

Think of it as your own little museum of the internet.


## Why I had the idea to make this

The internet has become very good at giving us things, but not always very good at letting us own them.

Offline-Internet is an experiment in doing the opposite.

Your music.
Your knowledge.
Your memories of the internet.
Your discoveries.

All in one place, controlled by you.
